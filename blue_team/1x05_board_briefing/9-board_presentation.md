### Part 1 - The One-Pager

#### Board Security Brief: Executive Summary

MedDefense Health Systems faces an immediate, verified cyber threat from the Crimson Tide ransomware affiliate group, which is actively targeting regional hospitals via CVE-2023-27997. Our security posture currently operates at a reactive baseline, burdened by a flat internal network, unencrypted databases, and an unpatched perimeter firewall that leaves our 350-bed acute care and 50,000 patient records exposed.

To neutralize this crisis, we have mobilized a mandatory 72-hour emergency action plan to renew our Fortinet support contract for $2,400, apply critical perimeter patches, and restrict administrative access vectors. Historically, our security spend has stalled at an unmanaged baseline; however, this emergency intervention directly protects an annualized loss exposure of $4,750,000.

We request immediate Board authorization for our baseline budget alongside an emergency supplemental allocation to fund urgent segmentation and monitoring controls. Implementing this structured framework ensures clinical continuity, regulatory compliance, and the absolute safeguarding of patient lives.
