## 0. The Crypto Inventory

### Executive Overview

This document presents a comprehensive **Data Protection Map** for MedDefense Health Systems, directly synthesizing our infrastructure inventory, vulnerability findings, and Sarah Park's cryptographic audit notes. By crossing 7 core data categories against 3 data states (At Rest, In Transit, In Use), this matrix makes our security gaps visible, fulfilling the operational requirements for Phase 1 of our security strategy.

### Gap Summary & Quantitative Coverage Analysis

Across the 21 primary data state cells evaluated in the matrix:

* **Adequate:** **3 / 21 cells** (14.3%) — Specifically O365 email storage/transit and enterprise site-to-site VPN tunnels.
* **Weak:** **5 / 21 cells** (23.8%) — Database transit paths, Active Directory storage/protocols, and Westside VPN endpoint trust.
* **Absent:** **13 / 21 cells** (61.9%) — Unencrypted databases at rest, unencrypted PACS medical storage/DICOM transit, plaintext NAS backups, and active memory processing vulnerabilities.

**Overall Crypto Coverage Percentage:** **14.3% Adequate Coverage** (with 85.7% of operational data states exhibiting significant vulnerability or total absence of cryptographic protection). This baseline underscores the urgency of executing Phase 1 remediation across MedDefense infrastructure.
