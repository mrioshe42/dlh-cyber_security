Policy number: MED-POL-2026-001 | Title: Acceptable Use of MedDefense Systems
Effective: 2026-11-01 | Owner: IT Security | Approved by: CISO

**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, temporary staff, vendors with system access.


## Policy

**MedDefense systems exist for authorized patient care and business only. Personal use is prohibited.**

### Prohibited

**Patient records:** Do not access outside active care relationship. No family, friends, colleagues, or public figures unless clinically justified.

**Credentials:** Never share passwords. Never allow another person to use your account. Each person gets their own account.

**Software/devices:** No unauthorized installation. No personal USB/external drives without IT approval. No personal devices on clinical network without enrollment.

**Network:** Always use VPN when remote (no public Wi-Fi without VPN). Lock or log out when away.

**Data:** Do NOT send patient data via personal email, Google Drive, Dropbox, WhatsApp, or other non-BAA services. Do NOT paste into ChatGPT or external tools. Do NOT export to personal devices.

**Access:** Do not access systems/data/networks beyond your assigned role. No admin access without authorization.

**Other:** No personal business use, illegal content, harassment, circumventing security controls, or actions that disrupt care.

### Remote Access Requirements
- VPN mandatory when remote
- Personal devices must be enrolled in MDM
- Laptop encryption required
- Screen privacy required (no visible to others)

### Reporting Obligations
**Report immediately** (not end of shift):
- Suspicious email or system behavior
- Lost/stolen devices
- Accidental PHI disclosure
- Credential compromise
- Policy violations by others

**Report to:** IT Security ticket (mark "Security Incident") | Urgent: [phone] | Confidential: [CISO email]

### Consequences
- Immediate access revocation
- Termination of employment
- Loss of clinical privileges
- Personal HIPAA liability: $100 per violation per day + criminal charges
- OCR reporting required
- Referral to law enforcement

**Note:** Personal liability applies even if MedDefense doesn't discipline you.
