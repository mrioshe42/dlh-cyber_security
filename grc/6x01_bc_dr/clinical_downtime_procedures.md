# MedDefense Clinical Downtime Procedures

**Version:** 1.0 | **Prepared by:** Clinical Operations | **Date:** November 3, 2026

## Epic EHR Downtime

**Declare When:** Login fails >5 min on multiple workstations. Call IT (ext 4357) to confirm.  
**Who Declares:** Charge Nurse / Lab Manager / Respiratory Manager  
**Announce:** **"Epic Downtime Activated"** overhead

| Step | Action | Form Location | Contact |
|------|--------|---|---|
| 1. **Patient ID [CRITICAL]** | Verify photo ID + DOB. Write name/MRN on ALL forms. Use temp ID if MRN unknown. | ED registration desk | — |
| 2. **Medications [CRITICAL]** | **Call Pharmacy (ext 5200) for allergy verification BEFORE giving any med.** Document on MAR. | Supply room, Drawer B | Pharmacy 5200 |
| 3. Lab Orders | Call Lab (ext 5100). Provide: name, MRN, test, specimen type, bed. Write on form; send with patient. | Lab collection, shelf 2 | Lab 5100 |
| 4. Imaging | Call Radiology (ext 5400). Write request; provide verbal history. | Nursing station, drawer 3 | Radiology 5400 |
| 5. Physician Orders | Write on order form. **Deliver directly to provider in person.** Physician signs. | Nursing station, folder | — |
| 6. Vital Signs | Record time/name/MRN/vitals on sheet. **Alert provider immediately if abnormal.** | Supply cabinet, folder | — |

**Escalation:** Charge Nurse → Nursing Director (5050) → CNO (5010) → Incident Commander (5555)

**After Restoration (1 hour):** Enter all meds/labs/orders into Epic. Reconcile temp IDs. Scan paper forms.

## Laboratory System Downtime

**Declare When:** Lab system fails on multiple workstations. Call IT (ext 4357).  
**Who Declares:** Lab Manager / Charge Technician  
**Notify:** All nursing units by phone (NOT email): **"Lab System Downtime Activated"**

| Step | Action | Form Location | Contact |
|------|--------|---|---|
| 1. **Orders [CRITICAL]** | Nursing calls Lab (ext 5100). Provide: name, MRN, test, specimen type, bed. Lab repeats back to confirm. | Lab reception, blue bin | Lab 5100 |
| 2. **Specimen [CRITICAL]** | Label tube: name, MRN, date/time, initials. **Send paper order form with specimen.** | Lab collection | — |
| 3. Results | Lab calls unit directly (NOT email) when ready. Unit documents result. | Nursing station | — |
| 4. **Critical Values [CRITICAL]** | Lab calls provider (pager/phone). Lab calls unit to confirm. **Unit documents time of both calls.** | Paper contact list | — |
| 5. Specimen Log | Log all specimens: name, MRN, type, time, initials. Prevents loss/tracking issues. | Lab reception, ledger | — |

**Escalation:** Lab Manager → Nursing Director (5050) → CMO (ext 5020) if backlog >2 hrs. CMO authorizes **Priority Testing Only** (stat/critical; defer routine).

**After Restoration (2 hours):** Enter results into lab system. Verify critical results acted upon. Scan forms.

## Quick Reference

**Contacts:** IT 4357 | Pharmacy 5200 | Lab 5100 | Radiology 5400 | Nursing Director 5050 | CNO 5010 | CMO 5020 | Incident Commander 5555

**Paper Form Locations:** Nursing Station (Downtime folder) | Supply Room (Drawer B/C) | Lab Reception (Blue bin) | ED Registration (Cabinet)

**Critical Rule:** Paper forms = legal documentation of care during downtime. Do NOT rely on memory. Write everything. Scan after restoration.

**Patient safety is the priority. When uncertain, ask your supervisor.**
